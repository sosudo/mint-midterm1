Measure & Integration

The handwritten lecture notes in this repository, typeset as an interactive textbook. The text is transcribed from the notes; on top of it you can select any proof step for an explanation of why it holds, listen to a narrated walk-through of each lecture, and watch animated explainers of the key ideas.

Narration uses a natural neural voice that ships with the site as audio files, so it works offline once loaded. You can switch to your device's own voice (Web Speech API) in the ⚙ menu of the player bar — that voice varies between Mac, iPhone, Chrome, etc.

  1. 1History, Jordan vs. Lebesgue, and the Riemann IntegralWhy measure theory exists; elementary sets, Jordan and Lebesgue measurability; Riemann sums and the limits of the Riemann integral.
  2. 2Limits of Riemann Integrals and Outer MeasuresLimits of Riemann integrable functions; the goal of the Lebesgue integral; outer measures: Lebesgue, Lebesgue–Stieltjes, Hausdorff; Props 2.7–2.11.
  3. 3Outer Measure of Rectangles and a Non-Measurable SetRecap of the outer-measure recipe; proof that the outer measure of a rectangle is its volume; translation invariance; the Vitali set and failure of additivity.
  4. 4Carathéodory Measurability, σ-Algebras and Borel SetsNo translation-invariant countably additive length on all subsets; Carathéodory's criterion; σ-algebras, generated σ-algebras, Borel sets; metric outer measures make Borel sets measurable.
